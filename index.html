Paperwork privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This policy covers Paperwork for Android (com.sashstore.paperwork), published by Small Digits.

Your documents

Paperwork processes PDFs and images on your device. The app has no document-upload service or user accounts. PDF and image contents are not supplied to the advertising SDK. Selected documents are held in memory while you work. Closing or clearing the workspace releases application references; this is not a guarantee of secure memory erasure.

Saving and sharing

When you choose Save, Android asks you to select a location. When you choose Share, Android asks you to select another app. Cloud storage providers and receiving apps may upload or otherwise process files you choose to send; their own privacy policies apply. Paperwork does not control those services.

Temporary copies

Exports are temporarily written to private app cache. Save-related copies are deleted when the save finishes or is cancelled. Copies used for sharing are retained so the receiving app can read them, and copies older than 24 hours are cleaned up on a later app launch or export. Failed or interrupted exports may also remain until that cleanup. Android may clear cached files earlier. Uninstalling Paperwork removes its app cache. Files you exported remain in the chosen location until you delete them.

File access

You choose individual files using Android's file picker. The app does not request broad access to all your files, contacts, location, microphone or camera. File picker providers may have their own access requirements.

Banner advertising

The banner-enabled Android build includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. Earlier builds without these SDKs do not request ads. Development builds use Google test ads, which do not generate revenue. Ads require an internet connection; PDF processing works offline.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including advertising ID where available, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This data is encrypted in transit. Paperwork does not send your selected documents, filenames or document text as ad targeting data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

In live-ad builds, required consent messages appear before ads are requested. Where available, use the Ad privacy choices control to change your choices. Android settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Consent choices may affect the ads shown; they do not restrict PDF tools. Google manages retention of advertising data under its policies; uninstalling Paperwork does not itself delete information already received by Google.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending confidential documents. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and kept only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise the date above.
